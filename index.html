    Install on iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this app’s link in Safari.
    2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
    3. Keep Open as Web App on, if shown, then tap Add.
    4. Open the new Pocket Prompter icon once while online.

    Preparing offline access…

    Install first, then create your scripts inside the installed app. Safari and the Home Screen app may keep separate data; use a script backup to move your work.

    Your first recording

    Open a script, tap Record video and allow the camera and microphone. Keep the phone upright or sideways as you want the final video. Tap Record; the countdown gives you time to get ready.

    The words appear near the camera for you to read. They are not added to your video. Use Pause to pause the words and the recording together. Optional silence assistance waits for sound without pausing the video. Finish opens your take. On iPhone, tap Save to Photos, then choose Save Video in the share menu. Download file saves to Files instead; the app cannot add a video to Photos without your action in the system menu.

    Stay in the app while recording. A call, screen lock or app switch may interrupt the camera; the app stops and attempts to save what it has received.

    Control the words

    Adjust words per minute, text size, line spacing, reading width, colours and countdown in Reading settings. Drag the text to find your place. Mirroring is available for teleprompter glass.

    With a keyboard or compatible Bluetooth clicker: Space = play/pause, arrows = move or change speed, Home = restart, Escape = leave. In recording mode Space pauses an existing take; use the Record button to begin.

    Keep a copy

    Scripts save automatically on this device. Import TXT, Markdown, Word (.doc and .docx), text-based PDF and RTF files. Scanned PDFs need text recognition elsewhere first.

    Backups contain scripts and reading settings, not videos. Use Save or Share on each take for videos. There are no app-imposed script or recording time limits; available memory and device storage still matter.

    Private, and free

    No ads, analytics, accounts, subscriptions or watermark. Reading, recording and importing run on your device. After the offline download is ready, these features work without a connection.

    Optional voice following uses your browser’s speech service. It may send audio to its provider and need internet. It is experimental and separate from video recording. It only starts if you choose it.

    Optional English automatic captions use a separate, free download and process your recording on this device. This app does not include AI script writing, eye-contact correction, cloud sync or a floating window over other iPhone apps.

    Automatic captions · English

    Turn the speech in your selected start/end interval into captions. The optional tools download about 66 MB once. Audio stays on this device; there is no account or charge. Keep the app open while it works. Cancel can take a moment to respond.

    Download the optional caption tools once, then use them offline.
